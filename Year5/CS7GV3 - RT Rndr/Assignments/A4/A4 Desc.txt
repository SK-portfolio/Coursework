The scene renders a 2D quad as a ground plane, displaying the visual effects while applying nearest-neighbour, linear, and MIP-mapped filters to different textures. These textures include those used in the last assignment, such as brick, metal, and stone, as well as a black-and-white chequerboard texture to accentuate the effect of each filter on the texture. The camera can be moved around the quad using mouse input to see the filtering effect on the textured quad from different perspectives. In addition to the other external libraries used in earlier assignments (OpenGL (3.3 core), GLFW, GLM, Assimp, etc.), to demonstrate how anisotropic filtering impacts MIP-mapped textures, GL_TEXTURE_MAX_ANISOTROPY_EXT and GL_MAX_TEXTURE_MAX_ANISOTROPY_EXT were defined using the GLAD library.
