The scene renders a 2D quad, which showcases the diffuse and normal mapping of various textures. These textures include brick, metal, and stone, all portraying a sense of depth and realism using a mouse cursor-directed light source. The intensity and radius of said light source can be controlled via keyboard input. The camera can be moved around the quad with mouse input to view the lights' effect on the textured quad from various angles. To demonstrate the normal mapped textures in a 3D environment, the quad can be swapped out for a rotating cube model during runtime. External libraries used in the development of this program include OpenGL (3.3 core) and several third-party libraries, including GLFW for windowing and input, GLAD for OpenGL function loading, GLM for mathematics, Assimp for model loading, stb_image for texture and cubemap loading, and GLText for on-screen text rendering.
