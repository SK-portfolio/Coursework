The scene renders a rotating 3D model which showcases physically inspired transmission and reflection effects. These effects include refraction, Fresnel-based reflectance, and chromatic dispersion, all influenced by an environment cubemap that serves as a skybox and controlled via keyboard input. The camera can be moved around the object with mouse input, allowing reflective and refractive qualities to be studied from various perspectives, and several meshes can be swapped in at runtime. External libraries used in the development of this program include OpenGL (3.3 core) and several third-party libraries, including GLFW for windowing and input, GLAD for OpenGL function loading, GLM for mathematics, Assimp for model loading, stb_image for texture and cubemap loading, and GLText for on-screen text rendering.
